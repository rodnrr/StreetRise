# StreetRise AI blog quality checklist

Before publishing an AI-generated draft, confirm:

- Each section adds new information rather than repeating prior paragraphs.
- Event date, time, address, phone number, and registration link are not repeated unnecessarily.
- `##` headings create a clear visual hierarchy.
- Paragraphs are short enough to scan comfortably on mobile.
- Important logistics use selective `**bold**` emphasis.
- Lists are used for details or steps where they improve readability.
- Supplied URLs are descriptive Markdown links rather than bare URLs.
- Supplied email addresses are clickable `mailto:` links.
- No invented URLs, contacts, partnerships, statistics, dates, availability, or quotes appear.
- No hero prompt, excerpt, SEO notes, keywords, or generation instructions leaked into the article body.
- The generated post remains unpublished until an admin reviews it.
